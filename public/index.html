Connect your workspace

Configure integrations in the server’s .env file, then restart. Credentials stay on the server.

  1. Set AI_MODE=live and OPENAI_API_KEY for LLM classification.
  2. Add SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL for your Slack destination.
  3. Add a Google service account and GOOGLE_SHEET_ID. Share the Sheet with the service account.
  4. Set LIVE_DELIVERY=true to enable configured destinations. Approval is still required.
  5. Import n8n/triage-workflow.json into n8n to orchestrate webhook ingestion and classification.
